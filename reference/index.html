STAGE 2 · LEARN

The Paraphrasing Studio

What is paraphrasing?

Expressing an existing idea in different words and sentence structure, without changing its meaning.

Why? Use sources, show understanding, avoid too many quotes. A paraphrase still needs a citation.

1 Understand

Find the central idea.

2 Reword

Choose alternatives.

3 Restructure

Rebuild the sentence.

Watch the transformation

ORIGINAL

Regular participation in university clubs can help students develop communication skills and establish social connections.

PARAPHRASED

By taking part in campus clubs, students can improve their ability to communicate and build relationships with others.

Notice: “participation” → “taking part”; “develop” → “improve”; and the sentence now begins with “By taking part…”. The meaning is intact.
